The feature worktree is `/root/worktrees/dsh-file-viewer-016`; integration Host and `/root/dsh-file-viewer` are read-only references. No live profile or service is changed.

User-owned dirty changes in `/root/dsh-file-viewer` supplied the capability/recommendation/detection split, resource/type associations, bounded Range prefix reads, MPEG-TS/BDAV detection and full-height HTML preview. Their September 13 handler-detection and manual-availability logs establish why filename exceptions and metadata-only manual availability are unsuitable. These changes are migrated with the official sidebar adapter, not replaced with a `.ts` exception.

The official navigation domain requires exact transaction identity. Wrapping its `current` method would fail placement ownership checks; passing the unguarded original would permit a late source/handler completion. The approved Host seam adds `cancel(reason)` to the owned transaction. Viewer forwards registration-lifetime cancellation while awaiting guarded placement and removes its abort listener afterward.
